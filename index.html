ℹ️ Where the calorie figures come from, and how accurate they are
  • Foods on the list: average estimates for one typical serving, based on the Malaysian Food Composition Database (MyFCD), Ministry of Health Malaysia (KKM) calorie guides and USDA FoodData Central.
  • Actual calories can differ by about 20–30% depending on the shop, recipe, serving size, and the oil and sugar used.
  • ⭐ My foods: values come from an AI estimate, an estimate by food type, or a number you entered. They are not official data. For packaged foods, the nutrition label (“Energy”) is the most accurate source.
  • Activity times: calculated from the Compendium of Physical Activities for an adult of about 60 kg. Lighter or heavier people burn energy at different rates.
  • The 2,000 kcal daily reference is a general value used on nutrition labels. Real needs depend on age, sex, body size and activity level.

🧭 Eating tips for different situations

🏃 Before / After Exercise

  • Before: Simple carbohydrates (banana/toast) 30–60 minutes ahead for quick energy.
  • After: A mix of protein & carbohydrates (chicken/egg + rice) for muscle recovery.

📚 Exam / Study Time

  • Brain focus: Omega-3-rich foods (fish, nuts) and fresh fruit.
  • Avoid drowsiness: Go easy on very heavy meals & high-sugar drinks while studying.

🤒 Unwell / Sensitive Stomach

  • Easy to digest: Chicken porridge, egg soup, or plain toast without butter.
  • Hydration: Warm water, coconut water, or soup to prevent dehydration.

⏱️ Busy Day / Short on Time

  • Quick healthy snacks: Egg/tuna sandwich, a banana, or nuts.
  • Fast choices: Pick steamed, boiled or soup versions over fried ones.

📚 Quick notes for students

🥦 Variety

Eat a variety of foods so your body gets a range of nutrients.

💧 Water

Plain water is the easiest way to meet your body's fluid needs.

🏃 Move

Walking, sports, cycling and dancing all count as physical activity.

😴 Sleep

Enough sleep matters for learning, energy and recovery.

🍰 Favourite Foods

Cake, chocolate and other favourites can be enjoyed in moderation, without guilt.

🧠 Mindful Eating

Eat slowly and pay attention to how hungry and full you feel.
